Mission

Foray Newfoundland and Labrador is a not-for-profit organization conducting amateur mushroom forays in the province of Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

Mission

To organize enjoyable and informative amateur mushroom forays in Newfoundland and Labrador and disseminate the knowledge gained.

Vision

A society which values the pleasure derived from the scientific, aesthetic, spiritual and gastronomic enjoyment of mushrooms as an important, interesting and integral part of nature, pursued in their natural setting in the company of peers with similar curiosity and interest.

Values

  • Make mushroom forays appealing and accessible to all at reasonable cost.
  • Strive for a sense of fellowship and camaraderie at forays and meetings.
  • Provide fodder for diverse interests of foray participants.
  • Identify and document Newfoundland and Labrador Mycota reliably.
  • Disseminate knowledge about mushrooms through forays, talks, writings, media.
  • Promote interest in mushrooms through forays, media.
  • Promote interest in mushrooms among youth by supporting the participation of children, youths and students at our forays.
  • Learn through review of our findings.
  • Teach through publication of our findings.
  • Share our findings, data and specimens with interested investigators worldwide.
