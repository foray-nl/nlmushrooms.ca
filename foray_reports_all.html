Foray Reports 2003-Present

Our foray reports document the events, faculty, participants and trails of each foray. They include the program, articles related to the foray, the species collected and their distribution along the trails, and a commentary on what was achieved and what was learned. They also list our partners, without whose support this work would not have happened.

All reports are downloadable PDF files and open in a new window. Since 2012 the reports have been published as special issues of Omphalina. The species lists by trail show which species have grown on each trail in previous years. For each foray's dates and place, see Past Forays.
